## Decisions needed before launch

Confirm delivery coverage, fees, lead time, closures, slots and capacity; product inventory and quantity policy; order status, cancellation, refunds and card-to-card reconciliation; canonical currency and gateway conversion; production SMS provider/template; payment provider sandbox/live agreement, callback domain and reconciliation; map provider terms, attribution and privacy; production host, domain, HTTPS, backups, retention, incident response and monitoring; and final responsive/accessibility approval. Password login is explicitly out of scope for now: customers sign in by OTP only. These are not defaults and should not be inferred from synthetic fixtures.

## Demonstrable paths and limitations

With an isolated local database and test fixtures, the current build can demonstrate the public catalog and information pages, mocked OTP UI, cart item edits and guest merge, address management, admin login/role isolation, product/category/media administration, customer management, content/SEO settings and contact message review. Delivery selection can be demonstrated with an explicitly synthetic policy; the review and manual-order pages are previews and never create orders or charge money. Order history/admin order management can display synthetic snapshots only. Do not present these as a real end-to-end purchase.

Operator-facing Persian instructions are in `operator-guide-fa.md`. Setup, environment, index, backup and rollback details are in `deployment-operations.md`; current security and provider limitations are in `security-reliability-review.md` and `journey-verification.md`. No production deployment, provider call, real customer data or live payment is authorized by this handoff.
